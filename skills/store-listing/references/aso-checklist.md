# App store optimisation (ASO) writing rules

## Title and subtitle
- Lead with the brand, follow with the top keyword: "Ledger - Expense Tracker".
- Do not repeat words between App Store name, subtitle and keywords; each slot is indexed separately.
- Avoid words the stores flag: free, best, top, #1, new, sale, download, Android/iPhone/Play/App Store, competitor names, "official" without being the official app.

## Short description / promotional text
- One sentence, benefit first, keyword second.
- Write for the person scanning search results on a phone.

## Full description
- Paragraph 1 (two to three lines) must stand alone: what it is, who it is for, why it is better.
- Then 5 to 8 feature bullets, each starting with a verb or the feature name, each a real feature in the code.
- Then a trust paragraph: offline support, privacy stance, no ads, open source, support email.
- If the app requests a sensitive permission, add a "Permissions" section with one line per permission explaining the user-facing reason. Reviewers read this.
- End with a call to action that does not say "download now".
- Keep sentences short; the stores do not render headings, so use blank lines and simple bullets (• or -).

## Keywords (App Store)
- 100 chars, comma-separated, no spaces after commas.
- Singular forms only (Apple matches plurals).
- No words already in name or subtitle.
- Mix 1 to 2 high-volume generic words with 6 to 10 specific ones.
- Localise per storefront; the English (US) field does not cover the UK or Australia.

## Screenshots (copy for captions)
- First screenshot carries the main promise in 3 to 5 words.
- One idea per screenshot.
- Show real UI; do not fake features.

## Compliance
- Do not promise features behind a paywall without saying so.
- Medical, financial or legal claims need disclaimers and often a licence.
- Age-appropriate language if the rating is for children.
- Localised listings must be real translations, not machine output pasted blind.
